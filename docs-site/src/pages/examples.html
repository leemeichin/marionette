Projects that suit Marionette

  • Agent-run projects with important decisions. The agent can only use choices in the plan, and choices marked @human always require your approval.
  • Migrations and launches. Write down fallback plans for problems such as mismatched data or a cancelled launch window. Marionette checks that every fallback leads somewhere.
  • Work with a retry limit. Express rules such as “try up to three times, then ask a person” directly in the plan.
  • Projects that need a decision record. Every choice stores who made it, when, and why. render shows the route taken, while summarize creates a status report.
  • Anything you'd sketch as a flowchart and then ignore. Marionette checks the flowchart, notices when it changes during a run, and lets you accept the update without losing the project history.
