Turn issues into pull requests

A compact agent workflow: pick an issue, update its status, implement it, open a pull request, and repeat. The counter makes this example finish after three issues; a real plan could use a human checkpoint or another explicit stopping rule instead.

Read it from top to bottom

VAR keeps progress. ~ prs_opened += 1 updates it when work starts. The + route may repeat, and ~loop~ confirms that the return is intentional.

Add limits and human decisions

The checkout plan allows up to five evidence-driven attempts. A person decides whether to launch, rethink, or stop. Try taking “Cohort converts” while acting as the agent: Marionette refuses because that route is marked @human.

Where Marionette fits

  • Agent-run projects with important decisions. @human keeps approval with a named person.
  • Migrations and launches. Write down rollback and fallback routes before they are needed.
  • Work with a retry limit. Try a bounded number of times, then stop or ask someone.
  • Projects that need a decision record. Every route records who took it, when, and why.
