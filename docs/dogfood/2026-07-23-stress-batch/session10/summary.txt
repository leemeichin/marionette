## Walkthrough

### bench_validation (start)
Bench-validate the Rev B PCB: power rails, sensor readings, interfaces, firmware bring-up. Done when the board meets the bench test checklist with no open hardware faults.
- **Bench validation passes** → emc_precompliance (repeatable)
- **Hardware fault needs a board respin** → board_respin (only if `respins < 2`; repeatable)
- **Fault found but respin budget is spent** → respin_review (only if `respins >= 2`; repeatable)

### emc_precompliance
EMC pre-compliance testing at the lab: radiated and conducted emissions, immunity scans. Done when the board passes with margin to spare.
- **Pre-compliance passes** → emc_certification (repeatable)
- **EMC failure, respin the board** → board_respin (only if `respins < 2`; repeatable)
- **EMC failure but respin budget is spent** → respin_review (only if `respins >= 2`; repeatable)

### emc_certification
Full EMC certification at the accredited lab. Done when the certificate is issued.
- **Certification passes** → bom_finalization (repeatable)
- **Certification failure, respin the board** → board_respin (only if `respins < 2`; repeatable)
- **Certification failure but respin budget is spent** → respin_review (only if `respins >= 2`; repeatable)

### board_respin
Respin the PCB: incorporate fixes, re-layout, fabricate and assemble a new rev. Each respin is about six weeks and real money.
- on entry: `respins += 1`
- **New rev in hand, back to the bench** → bench_validation (loops back; repeatable)

### respin_review
Two respins have been spent. Sit down with the hardware lead to decide whether another respin is worth the time and money before committing more.
- **Hardware lead funds another respin** → board_respin (**requires a human decision**; loops back; repeatable)
- **Halt the production effort** → END (**requires a human decision**; repeatable)

### bom_finalization
Firm up the bill of materials: lock part selections, confirm sourcing and lead times for every line item. In practice this runs in parallel with the EMC track. Done when the BOM is complete and the main MCU lead time is known.
- **MCU lead time is within twelve weeks** → cm_quotes (repeatable)
- **MCU lead time slips past twelve weeks** → alternate_mcu_redesign (repeatable)

### alternate_mcu_redesign
Redesign the board around the alternate MCU instead of waiting out the lead time. The new rev must re-enter bench validation and the EMC track.
- **Redesign complete, new rev in hand** → bench_validation (loops back; repeatable)

### cm_quotes
Request and compare quotes from two contract manufacturers for the 500-unit run: unit cost, NRE, tooling, lead time, quality terms. Done when both quotes are in hand and compared.
- **Quotes in hand and compared** → final_approval (repeatable)

### final_approval
The hardware lead reviews and approves the final BOM and the contract manufacturer choice.
- **BOM and CM approved** → place_production_order (**requires a human decision**; repeatable)
- **Changes requested, rework the BOM or quotes** → bom_finalization (**requires a human decision**; loops back; repeatable)

### place_production_order
Place the 500-unit production order with the chosen CM. This is the point of no return: once placed, the spend is committed.
- **Place the production order** → production_run (**requires a human decision**; repeatable)
- **Hold, revisit the BOM and quotes first** → bom_finalization (**requires a human decision**; loops back; repeatable)

### production_run
The CM builds and delivers the 500-unit production run. Done when units are received and accepted against the acceptance criteria.
- otherwise → END
