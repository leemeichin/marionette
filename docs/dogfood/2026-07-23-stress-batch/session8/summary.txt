## Walkthrough

### observability_build (start)
Build the new observability stack: metrics, logs, traces, dashboards and alerting on the PaaS.
Nothing else ships until this is live; without it we fly blind.
- **Stack live, dashboards and alerts verified** → auth_extraction

### auth_extraction
Extract the auth service from the monolith behind a strangler-fig facade.
Done means the new service can take auth calls in shadow while the monolith stays authoritative.
- **Extraction complete, start shadowing** → auth_shadow (repeatable)

### auth_shadow
Mirror production auth traffic to the new service and compare results.
Parity metrics must hold for two full weeks before cutover is considered.
- **Parity metrics held for two weeks** → auth_capacity_signoff (repeatable)
- **Parity drifting, rework the extraction** → auth_extraction (loops back; repeatable)

### auth_capacity_signoff
Platform SRE reviews capacity ahead of the auth cutover.
- **SRE signs off capacity** → auth_cutover (**requires a human decision**; repeatable)
- **SRE flags gaps, remediate and re-shadow** → auth_shadow (**requires a human decision**; loops back; repeatable)

### auth_cutover
Cut live auth traffic over to the new service and watch the error budget for one week.
Burning more than 20 percent of the error budget in week one triggers a cutback.
- **Week-one burn within error budget** → billing_extraction (repeatable)
- **Burned over 20 percent of error budget, cut back** → auth_cutback_fix (repeatable)

### auth_cutback_fix
Auth traffic is cut back to the monolith while the team fixes the regression.
- on entry: `auth_cutbacks += 1`
- **Fixed, re-run shadow parity** → auth_shadow (only if `auth_cutbacks < 3`; loops back; repeatable)
- **Third cutback, auth approach goes to the architecture board** → arch_board_review (only if `auth_cutbacks >= 3`; **requires a human decision**; repeatable)

### billing_extraction
Extract the billing service from the monolith behind a strangler-fig facade.
Done means shadow ledger entries are being written alongside the monolith ledger.
- **Extraction complete, start shadowing** → billing_shadow (repeatable)

### billing_shadow
Mirror production billing traffic, writing shadow ledger entries, and compare results.
Parity metrics must hold for two full weeks before reconciliation begins.
- **Parity metrics held for two weeks** → billing_reconciliation (repeatable)
- **Parity drifting, rework the extraction** → billing_extraction (loops back; repeatable)

### billing_reconciliation
Finance reconciles a full month of shadow ledger entries against the monolith ledger, to the penny.
Billing cannot cut over until this reconciliation passes.
- **Finance confirms the month reconciles to the penny** → billing_capacity_signoff (**requires a human decision**; repeatable)
- **Discrepancies found, fix and re-run a ledger month** → billing_shadow (**requires a human decision**; loops back; repeatable)

### billing_capacity_signoff
Platform SRE reviews capacity ahead of the billing cutover.
- **SRE signs off capacity** → billing_cutover (**requires a human decision**; repeatable)
- **SRE flags gaps, remediate and re-shadow** → billing_shadow (**requires a human decision**; loops back; repeatable)

### billing_cutover
Cut live billing traffic over to the new service and watch the error budget for one week.
Burning more than 20 percent of the error budget in week one triggers a cutback.
- **Week-one burn within error budget** → monolith_freeze_booking (repeatable)
- **Burned over 20 percent of error budget, cut back** → billing_cutback_fix (repeatable)

### billing_cutback_fix
Billing traffic is cut back to the monolith while the team fixes the regression.
- on entry: `billing_cutbacks += 1`
- **Fixed, re-run shadow parity** → billing_shadow (only if `billing_cutbacks < 3`; loops back; repeatable)
- **Third cutback, billing approach goes to the architecture board** → arch_board_review (only if `billing_cutbacks >= 3`; **requires a human decision**; repeatable)

### monolith_freeze_booking
Agree a change-freeze window with support for the monolith lift-and-shift.
If the window gets pulled later, we come back here and rebook, as many times as it takes.
- **Freeze window agreed and booked** → monolith_capacity_signoff (repeatable)

### monolith_capacity_signoff
Platform SRE reviews capacity for moving the remaining monolith onto the PaaS as-is.
- **SRE signs off capacity** → monolith_migration (**requires a human decision**; repeatable)
- **SRE flags gaps, remediate and rebook the window** → monolith_freeze_booking (**requires a human decision**; loops back; repeatable)

### monolith_migration
Lift-and-shift the remaining monolith onto the PaaS during the agreed freeze window.
- **Freeze window pulled by support, rebook it** → monolith_freeze_booking (loops back; repeatable)
- **Monolith live on the PaaS** → program_review (repeatable)

### arch_board_review
The architecture board reviews the failing service's extraction approach after its third cutback.
The board either approves a revised approach for another attempt or halts the programme.
- **Board approves a revised auth approach, restart that extraction** → auth_extraction (**requires a human decision**; loops back; repeatable)
- **Board approves a revised billing approach, restart that extraction** → billing_extraction (**requires a human decision**; loops back; repeatable)
- **Board halts the programme** → END (**requires a human decision**; repeatable)

### program_review
End-of-programme review of the old estate.
Decide whether to decommission immediately or keep it warm for a quarter as insurance.
- **Decommission the old estate** → END (**requires a human decision**)
- **Keep the old estate warm for a quarter** → keep_warm (**requires a human decision**)

### keep_warm
The old estate stays warm for one quarter as insurance while the PaaS beds in.
- **Insurance quarter elapsed, decommission the old estate** → END (**requires a human decision**)
