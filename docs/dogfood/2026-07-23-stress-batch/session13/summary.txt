## Walkthrough

### data_inventory (start)
Inventory personal data across all eleven teams: each team fills in the
data map. Done when every team has submitted a complete data map.
- **All eleven data maps received** → draft_schedule (repeatable)
- **Some teams have not responded** → chase_stragglers (repeatable)

### chase_stragglers
Legal chases the teams that have not yet filled in their data map.
- on entry: `chase_rounds += 1`
- **Chased — re-check responses** → data_inventory (only if `chase_rounds < 6`; loops back; repeatable)
- **Inventory stalled, human decides how to proceed** → data_inventory (only if `chase_rounds >= 6`; **requires a human decision**; loops back; repeatable)

### draft_schedule
Legal drafts the data-retention schedule from the completed inventory.
- **Draft ready for DPO review** → dpo_schedule_review (repeatable)

### dpo_schedule_review
The DPO reviews the retention schedule and either approves it or bounces
it back to legal for redrafting, as often as needed.
- **DPO approves the schedule** → implement_deletion (**requires a human decision**; repeatable)
- **DPO bounces it for redrafting** → draft_schedule (**requires a human decision**; loops back; repeatable)

### implement_deletion
Teams implement deletion jobs against the approved retention schedule.
Done when every team has deletion jobs ready for compliance review.
- **Deletion jobs implemented across teams** → compliance_review

### compliance_review
Per-team compliance review of the deletion jobs. Teams pass, fail for the
first time, or fail for a second time.
- **All teams pass review** → external_audit (repeatable)
- **Some teams failed their first review** → remediation (repeatable)
- **A team has failed review twice** → dpo_escalation (repeatable)

### remediation
Failing teams (and any external-audit findings) get remediation tasks,
then go back for re-review.
- on entry: `remediation_rounds += 1`
- **Remediation done — re-review the teams** → compliance_review (only if `remediation_rounds < 4`; loops back; repeatable)
- **Remediation not converging** → dpo_escalation (only if `remediation_rounds >= 4`; **requires a human decision**; repeatable)

### dpo_escalation
Teams that failed review twice go on the escalation list for the DPO,
who decides how to unblock them.
- **DPO resolves the escalation — back to re-review** → compliance_review (**requires a human decision**; loops back; repeatable)

### external_audit
External counsel performs the final audit once all teams have passed
compliance review.
- **Audit clean, no findings** → program_close (repeatable)
- **Audit findings raised** → remediation (loops back; repeatable)

### program_close
The DPO formally closes the GDPR data-retention cleanup program.
- **DPO formally closes the program** → file_report (**requires a human decision**)

### file_report
Legal files the compliance report with the board.
- **Compliance report filed with the board** → END
