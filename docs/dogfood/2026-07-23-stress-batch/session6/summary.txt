## Walkthrough

### spike_engine (start)
Evaluate the hosted search engine against our relevance corpus.
- **Relevance acceptable out of the box** → integrate
- **Relevance poor** → tune_ranking

### tune_ranking
Adjust analyzers, synonyms and boosts; re-run the relevance suite after each change.
- on entry: `tuning_rounds += 1`
- **Tune again** → tune_ranking (only if `tuning_rounds < 4`; loops back; repeatable)
- **Good enough** → integrate (repeatable)
- **Not converging, escalate** → qa_signoff (only if `tuning_rounds >= 4`; **requires a human decision**; repeatable)

### integrate
Wire the engine into the product behind a feature flag.
- **Integration complete, suite green** → qa_signoff (repeatable)

### qa_signoff
The QA lead reviews relevance quality and signs off.
- **Approved** → rollout (**requires a human decision**)
- **Needs more tuning** → tune_ranking (**requires a human decision**; loops back; repeatable)

### rollout
Gradual rollout to all tenants, watching search abandonment metrics.
- otherwise → END
