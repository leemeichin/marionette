## Walkthrough

### spike_sdk (start)
Spike the embedded-analytics SDK: throwaway prototype plus five customer discovery calls, two weeks max.
- **Prototype built and five discovery calls done** → spike_logistics
- **Two-week timebox expired, cut the spike** → spike_logistics

### spike_logistics
Spike the vertical product for logistics companies: throwaway prototype plus five customer discovery calls, two weeks max.
- **Prototype built and five discovery calls done** → spike_copilot
- **Two-week timebox expired, cut the spike** → spike_copilot

### spike_copilot
Spike the AI-native reporting copilot: throwaway prototype plus five customer discovery calls, two weeks max. This closes out the six-week total spiking budget.
- **Prototype built and five discovery calls done** → board_direction
- **Two-week timebox expired, cut the spike** → board_direction

### board_direction
The board reviews all three spike results and either picks a direction or kills the company.
- **Board picks the embedded-analytics SDK** → build_mvp (**requires a human decision**)
- **Board picks the logistics vertical** → build_mvp (**requires a human decision**)
- **Board picks the AI reporting copilot** → soc2_readiness (**requires a human decision**; repeatable)
- **Board kills the company** → END (**requires a human decision**)

### soc2_readiness
Copilot-only gate: complete a SOC 2 readiness review, since no design partner will touch the copilot without it.
- **Readiness review passed** → build_mvp
- **Readiness unachievable this quarter, send the direction choice back to the board** → board_direction (**requires a human decision**; loops back; repeatable)

### build_mvp
Build the MVP for the chosen direction. Done means a product that design partners can actually run.
- **MVP ready for design partners** → design_partner_beta

### design_partner_beta
Run a six-week design-partner beta; the goal is at least three paying design partners. Each pass through this phase is one beta round, and two extensions are the limit.
- on entry: `beta_rounds += 1`
- **Three or more paying design partners on board** → contract_negotiation (repeatable)
- **Short of three partners, extend the beta** → design_partner_beta (only if `beta_rounds < 3`; loops back; repeatable)
- **Two extensions used and still short of three partners** → board_kill_or_continue (only if `beta_rounds >= 3`; repeatable)

### board_kill_or_continue
After two beta extensions without three paying design partners, the board decides whether the company keeps going.
- **Board kills the company** → END (**requires a human decision**)
- **Board continues, run another beta round** → design_partner_beta (**requires a human decision**; loops back; repeatable)

### contract_negotiation
Convert paying design partners into a signed annual contract. The CEO signs every contract personally.
- **CEO signs an annual contract** → scale_up (**requires a human decision**)
- **Deal falls through, work the beta cohort again** → design_partner_beta (loops back; repeatable)

### scale_up
A signed annual contract is the win condition: scale the chosen direction.
- otherwise → END
