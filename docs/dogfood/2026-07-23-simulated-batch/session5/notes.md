# Session 5 notes (as given)

> Our B2B analytics startup is stalling and the board has given us one quarter to find a new direction. We have three candidate pivots: an embedded-analytics SDK, a vertical product for logistics companies, and an AI-native reporting copilot. Spike each one — a spike means a throwaway prototype plus five customer discovery calls, two weeks max per spike, and we can spend at most six weeks total on spiking across everything. After the spikes the board picks a direction or kills the company; if they pick one, we build an MVP, run a six-week design-partner beta with at least three paying design partners, and iterate — but if after two beta extensions we still can't get three paying partners, back to the board for a kill/continue decision. The copilot option has an extra wrinkle: it needs a SOC 2 readiness review before any design partner will touch it. Any path that reaches a signed annual contract is a win and we scale; the CEO signs every contract personally.
