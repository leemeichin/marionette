## Walkthrough

### spike_replication (start)
Spike pglogical-style replication tooling to prove MySQL-to-Postgres replication can be made reliable. Done means replication runs cleanly against a production-like dataset, or we conclude it cannot be made reliable within two attempts.
- on entry: `spike_attempts += 1`
- **Replication is reliable** → cutover_planning
- **Fiddly — adjust and retry the spike** → spike_replication (only if `spike_attempts < 2`; loops back; repeatable)
- **Replication cannot be made reliable** → dual_write_fallback (only if `spike_attempts >= 2`)

### dual_write_fallback
Fall back to dual writes at the application layer: writes go to both MySQL and Postgres until parity is proven. Slower but safer. Done means dual writes are live and data parity checks pass.
- **Dual writes working with verified parity** → cutover_planning
- **Dual-write approach also blocked — abandon migration for now** → END (**requires a human decision**)

### cutover_planning
Draft the cutover plan: sequencing, maintenance window, verification steps, and the rollback procedure to MySQL. Done means a written plan ready for DBA review.
- **Cutover plan drafted** → dba_signoff

### dba_signoff
Priya (DBA) reviews the cutover plan. Nothing touches production without her sign-off.
- **Priya approves the cutover plan** → cutover (**requires a human decision**)
- **Priya requests changes** → cutover_planning (**requires a human decision**; loops back; repeatable)

### cutover
Execute the cutover from MySQL to Postgres during the agreed maintenance window, following the signed-off plan. Done means Postgres is serving as the primary.
- **Cutover completed in the window** → monitor_48h
- **Cutover failed mid-window** → rollback_regroup

### monitor_48h
Watch error rates and key metrics for 48 hours after cutover. Done means 48 hours elapsed with error rates within normal bounds.
- **Error rates normal for 48 hours** → stability_week
- **Error rates spiked** → rollback_regroup

### rollback_regroup
Roll back to MySQL as the primary and regroup: capture what went wrong and revise the approach before any retry.
- **Retry with a revised cutover plan** → cutover_planning (**requires a human decision**; loops back; repeatable)
- **Abandon the migration** → END (**requires a human decision**)

### stability_week
Postgres runs as primary for a full week. Done means one week of stable operation with no regressions.
- **Stable for a full week** → decommission
- **Instability emerged during the week** → rollback_regroup

### decommission
Decommission the old MySQL primary: final backups, revoke write paths, and retire the hardware or instance.
- otherwise → END
